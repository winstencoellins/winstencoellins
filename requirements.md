# Premium Minimalist Portfolio Landing Page

## Objective

Create a premium personal portfolio website for a freelance Product Designer and Web Designer. The design should feel modern, sophisticated, minimalist, and highly polished, similar to award-winning portfolio websites.

The overall aesthetic should combine the visual quality of:
- Apple
- Linear
- Framer
- Awwwards-winning portfolios
- Modern SaaS landing pages

Focus on simplicity, strong typography, generous whitespace, and elegant layouts.

---

## Design Style

- Minimalist and professional
- Scandinavian-inspired design
- Clean visual hierarchy
- Large typography
- Lots of breathing room
- Soft gray background
- White cards and sections
- Rounded corners (12–16px)
- Subtle shadows
- Thin borders
- Smooth animations
- Modern grid system
- Elegant and premium feel

---
